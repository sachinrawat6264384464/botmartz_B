About the Role

You'll own retrieval-augmented generation systems end to end — from ingestion pipelines and hybrid search to reranking, evaluation and guardrails. Our clients run these systems in production for real support, legal and operations teams, so reliability and observability matter exactly as much as raw model performance.

This is a generalist-leaning engineering role at a small, fast-moving team: you'll talk to clients, design the architecture, write the code, and watch it run in production — often in the same week. You'll also have room to contribute to our open-source labs and speak at community events like AI Frontier.
