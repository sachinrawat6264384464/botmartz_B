# Botmartz Academy — Repositioning Strategy

## Relationship to the company

Keep `academy.botmartz.com` as a **separate subdomain/brand** (like Google → Google Developers) rather than merging it into the main site — but it must clearly read as the **talent/education arm of Botmartz**, not a standalone side business.

> Botmartz AI Solutions → Builds AI systems.
> Botmartz Academy → Trains AI engineers.
> Botmartz Community → Connects AI professionals.
> Botmartz Labs → Experiments with new AI ideas and open source.

2,000+ students taught is a genuine credibility asset — the fix is *framing*, not hiding it.

## Core reframe

- From: "Learn AI" / "Generative AI Engineering Bootcamp" / salary-bump-led marketing
- To: **"Become an AI Engineer who can design, build, and deploy production AI systems."**

This ages better than "Generative AI," which is becoming a generic/marketing term, and it mirrors the company's own positioning.

## Critique of the current landing page (as built)

Reads as a ₹30,000 course sales page, not the academy of an AI engineering company:
- Leads with price/duration/salary bump — too transactional, undermines a premium positioning
- "Average Salary Bump 40–60%" — remove; premium academies don't lead with salary
- Curriculum (Neural Nets → Attention → Transformers → Embeddings) is generic ML fundamentals, not aligned to Botmartz's actual service focus (RAG/Agents)
- Instructor billed as "AI Mentor" rather than "Founder, Botmartz AI Solutions" — loses the company connection
- No visible link to Botmartz's identity, GitHub, or engineering work — feels disconnected from the company brand
- No mention of *why* the academy exists

## Rebuild direction

**Hero:** "AI Engineering Accelerator — Learn to build production AI systems" (Enterprise RAG, AI Agents, AI-Assisted Development, PyTorch, LangGraph, FastAPI, Production Deployment) — not "Generative AI Engineering Bootcamp."

**Why this academy exists (add this section):**
> "Botmartz Academy exists because the AI industry doesn't need more prompt engineers — it needs AI engineers who can design, build, evaluate, and deploy production AI systems."

**Outcomes section (replace salary-bump stat):**
By the end of the cohort you'll have built: Enterprise RAG ✅, Multi-Agent System ✅, AI API ✅, Evaluation Pipeline ✅, Production Deployment ✅, Portfolio ✅.

**Curriculum restructure:**
1. AI Engineering Foundations
2. LLMs
3. Enterprise RAG
4. AI Agents (LangGraph, Google ADK)
5. PyTorch
6. AI-Assisted Development (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot)
7. Production AI (FastAPI, Docker, deployment, evaluation)
8. Capstone — Enterprise AI System

**Instructor section:** "Founder & AI Engineer, Botmartz AI Solutions" — not generic "AI Mentor" — and add a visible "Built by Botmartz" signal so the academy is legible as the company's education arm.

**Naming:** Consider "Accelerator" or "Fellowship" over "Bootcamp" — stronger premium positioning.

**Program growth path over time:**
Botmartz Academy → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Enterprise → Corporate training — i.e., the Academy should be able to scale alongside the company rather than being frozen as one fixed course.

## 6-month priority level

Academy stays running (don't pause your cohorts — proven revenue and credibility), but keep it a **supporting pillar**, not a primary nav item, while the main site's prominent real estate stays focused on Enterprise RAG + AI Agents client work. Elevate Academy to a top-level pillar once the engineering brand/portfolio is stronger.
